# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, identity, experience, or background.

## Our standards

Be kind and constructive. Respect different viewpoints. Accept feedback gracefully. Do not harass, insult, discriminate against, or share private information about anyone.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that do not meet these standards and may block contributors who engage in abusive behavior. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
